K-nearest neighbors classification

Penguin Specimen Classification

A concise statistical consulting exercise identifying an unlabeled penguin specimen from bill and flipper measurements.

The problem

Given a specimen with a 45 mm bill and a 220 mm flipper, which species is the most likely match among Adelie, Chinstrap, and Gentoo penguins?

Approach

Bill length and flipper length were selected because they were available for the unknown specimen. Incomplete rows were removed, predictors were standardized to equalize their influence on distance, and KNN was applied with 15 neighbors.

Results

The unknown specimen fell within the Gentoo cluster in the feature plot, and all 15 nearest neighbors were Gentoo. This strongly supported a Gentoo classification while recognizing that the neighbor proportion is not literal certainty.

Limitations and next steps

The model uses only two measurements and a limited reference dataset. External validation, cross-validated selection of K, and additional morphological predictors would strengthen generalizability.

Project snapshot

  • 342 complete observations
  • 3 penguin species
  • K = 15 nearest neighbors
  • 15 of 15 neighbors were Gentoo
