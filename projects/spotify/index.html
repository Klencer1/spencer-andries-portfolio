Clustering and Profiling Spotify Songs

A large-scale analysis of Spotify audio features designed to identify natural song profiles beyond traditional genre labels.

The problem

Can songs be grouped into meaningful audio profiles using measurable characteristics such as danceability, energy, acousticness, instrumentalness, valence, and tempo?

Approach

The workflow cleaned and standardized the data, explored feature distributions and correlations, reduced dimensionality with PCA, and compared multiple clustering strategies. A PyTorch autoencoder was also used to learn a compact latent representation before K-Means clustering.

Results

The analysis identified interpretable profiles including high-energy/danceable music, speech-heavy tracks, acoustic songs, and instrumental music. Autoencoder-based clustering produced the strongest separation among the tested approaches.

Limitations and next steps

Cluster quality remained moderate, reflecting overlap among musical characteristics. Popularity was summarized after clustering rather than used to create the groups, and future work could compare additional embeddings or density-based clustering.

Project snapshot

  • 111,774 songs after filtering
  • 9 standardized audio features
  • PCA, K-Means, hierarchical clustering, autoencoder
  • Best silhouette score: 0.290
